# Pair a device

Detent only opens devices you've paired, and remembers them across restarts.

1. Plug the device in. The menu bar shows **New device found: Pair…**; choose it, or open Detent and click **Pair new device** on Devices.
2. **Press any button on the device** you mean. That proves which physical device it is when more than one is plugged in. On a device with an OLED you can also pick **Setup** in its menu, which shows its serial.
3. **Name it.** The name starts as the model's, such as *Detent Dev*.
4. **Done.** Tick the box to start from the model's starter mappings, or leave it to start empty.

The device's LED turns solid once it's paired.

## Devices

The Devices screen lists each paired device with its model, firmware version and a dot that's green while it's connected. From there you can rename or unpair it; unpairing also removes its mappings, and asks first.

A Detent device you don't want Detent to offer again can be **ignored**. Ignored devices are listed at the bottom and can be un-ignored.

If Devices says a device's firmware is too old, flash the current firmware: the device stays connected meanwhile, but Detent won't map it.

Unplugging and plugging back in, sleeping and waking the Mac all reconnect on their own.
