# The device menu

A device with an encoder and an OLED has a menu. **Long press the encoder's push switch** to open it from any mode, turn to move, press to pick, and long press again to close it.

The first items belong to the device:

- **Volume**, **Mapped**: switch the encoder's mode.
- **Setup**: pairing mode, showing the device's serial.
- **Device info**: firmware and protocol versions.

After them come up to eight items of your own. Edit them under **Device menu** on the Mappings screen: each is a short label (up to 16 characters) and one action, and you can drag them into order. Picking one on the device runs its action, the same as a mapping would, Pause and Dry run included.

With Detent not running, only the device's own items show.
